🤖 How MI works
The MI engine pulls real market data — prices, market cap & rankings from CoinMarketCap and OHLCV candles from Binance — then computes EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR and volume confluence on every cycle. Signals, confidence, take-profit and stop-loss levels are derived from those calculations, then cross-checked by the OpenRouter AI assistant.
⚠️ Important notice
MI is an analytical tool, not financial advice. Markets carry risk of loss. Always do your own research and use stop-losses and position sizing that fit your own risk tolerance. AI output can contain errors.
🗂️ Data sources
Prices, market cap & rankings — CoinMarketCap. Chart candles — Binance public market data (spot). Signals & analysis — MI engine (on-server). AI answers — OpenRouter. Notifications are delivered inside the app via your own MI permission.
